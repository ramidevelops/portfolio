A little more about me

Ramon Villamediana
Visual Designer · Design Engineer

I'm Ramon, a visual designer who likes working in the space between a sharp idea and the thing that actually ships. My work moves across brand systems, digital products, websites, campaigns, and the frontend code that brings them to life.

How I work

I care about the details that make something feel considered: the rhythm of a layout, the clarity of a sentence, the behavior of a component, and the last ten percent of polish. I like getting close to the problem, asking direct questions, and making work that is both expressive and useful.

Outside the brief

I'm naturally curious and a little hands-on. I learn by making, whether that's exploring a new tool, building a small interface, or finding a better way to explain a complicated idea. Good work, to me, should leave people with a clearer path forward.
