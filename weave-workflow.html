My Role

Solo build. I designed and built the pipeline on my own, and I maintain it.

The Problem

Turning the Mosaic social calendar into finished posts meant working through every entry by hand: sourcing imagery, drafting copy, looping in collaborators and applying brand rules. A full calendar took 2 days, on a recurring cycle with a lean team.

2d Previous turnaround per calendar
4h Turnaround after workflow
1 Person. Solo build & maintenance
The Solution

Mosaic's business partners aren't designers, so a template wasn't an option. They write the content in a Word document and I handle the design. A Figma Weave AI pipeline digests the social calendar and creates an on-brand asset for each entry, so I can iterate fast and send assets for approval.

How It Works

I turn the Word document into structured JSON (headline, body copy, CTA per entry) and feed it to Weave, which sorts each entry by post type: community investment, operations, recruiting, sustainability and more. Each type maps to a set background treatment.

Brand Guardrails

The pipeline is trained on Mosaic's brand guidelines, approved typography, color system and a curated reference library. I'm in charge of catching off-brand output. The success rate sits at about 90%: out of 100 assets, roughly 10 come out off brand.

Output Examples

Generated assets across post categories.

Result

Processing the calendar and designing every post dropped from 2 days to 4 hours. The time saved went back into higher-priority design work.
