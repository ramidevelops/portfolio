My Role

Design lead, every cycle. I direct, design and hand off every asset. Investor Relations and Finance supply the copy and the numbers, and I own the rest of the process.

The Cycle

Results release on a fixed date, and every asset goes live in sync the moment they do, with no room for delay. Each cycle: about 12 social assets, 1 internal email to 13,000 employees, 1 investor deck and updates to 3 websites. I also coordinate Spanish translations for South America.

The Design Work

One direction. Assets used to be made in silos, so the investor deck and internal comms looked different every cycle. I took the initiative to lead design direction on every asset.

Theme first. Each cycle starts with the theme IR wants to highlight (supply chain, operations, safety or a market highlight). Everything is built from it.

Deck template. I created a template made for reporting earnings, with upgraded visuals and data visualization so the charts and graphics are easy for anyone to read.

Internal email. The old one was a block of dense tables with weak hierarchy, and it wasn't telling the story to 13,000 employees across the globe. I rebuilt it with clear hierarchy and data visualization, so complex financial numbers are easy to digest.

Result. The deck, email, social and web now read as one set each cycle.
