Earnings & Investor Communications

Design Lead — Investor & Financial Communications
Mosaic · Fortune 500
PrintBrand

Design lead for Mosaic's quarterly earnings and investor communications — a recurring, high-stakes cycle covering everything from web design and press release publishing to social assets, internal emails, and investor deck data visualization.

As a public company, quarterly reporting runs on a fixed, non-negotiable timeline. Every asset — web, social, internal comms, investor materials — has to launch in sync the moment results are released, with zero room for delay.

The real challenge was translating dense financial data into something clear and visually compelling, while finding ways to spotlight genuine business wins even in a turbulent market. I collaborated closely with Investor Relations and Finance to shape how the data was told visually, managed a large volume of assets moving simultaneously, and coordinated Spanish translations for South America materials.
